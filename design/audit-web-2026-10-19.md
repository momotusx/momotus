# Web design audit, 2026-10-19

Every web screen captured headless on live data (the e2e core: Cinemeta, OpenSubtitles, Torrentio, the sample source) at TV 1920x1080, desktop 1440x900 and phone 390x844, then read against `design/DECISION.md` (Ember). Shots come from `apps/web/e2e/shots.spec.ts` with `SHOTS_DIR` and `SHOTS_PREFIX=audit-`; the after set is `apps/web/e2e/shots/audit-<screen>-<size>.jpg`. Sync and pairing were out of scope (under review).

Each screen lists its issues from the one that hurts the first impression most down, with what a top product (Netflix, Apple TV app, Disney+, flystream.net) does instead. Status: **fixed** (in this pass), **left** (ranked in the last section).

## Cross-screen

1. **Content slides under the floating navigation pill with nothing behind it** (Calendar opens this way; Home, Discover, Library, Settings once scrolled). Text sits on the same line as the pill and collides with it. Netflix and Disney+ put a scrim behind the top bar as soon as the page scrolls; flystream blurs. **Fixed**: a page-colored scrim fades in behind the pill once the page scrolls, and out at the top.
2. **Selected filter chips are white pills**, the look DECISION reserves for the one primary action. Discover shows three white pills at once (type, catalog, genre), Library two, Find addons two, Settings five. Apple TV and Netflix mark a selected filter with a lighter fill, never the primary button style. **Fixed**: a selected chip is a lighter fill with a bright edge and the check, the same as the season tabs.
3. **Focus can land under a fade or on the bottom edge on TV.** The episode list and the versions list fade their last 48 to 56 px, and a focused row there shows a cut ring; Settings opens with its focused row half below the screen. TV apps keep the focused item clear of the safe area. **Fixed**: focus scrolling honours a bottom scroll margin (the 60 px TV safe area, more above a fade).
4. **Raw colors outside the tokens**: the whole player (see Player), the browse title gradient end `#B9BDC6`. **Fixed** for both.
5. **Card edges are not one system**: link rows, addon cards and the facts table use the 1 px glass `edge`; the welcome doors and the versions pick card use a 2 px `line`, which reads as a different, flatter card. **Fixed**: one 1 px `edge` with the glass highlight.

## Home and Home rows

1. **The hero's eyebrow chip sits 17 px under the navigation pill on TV and desktop, and slides under it** when the synopsis runs to three lines (the hero stack is 482 px in a 600 px card whose top 100 px the pill covers). Netflix and flystream leave clear air between the top bar and the hero text. **Fixed**: the logo box is 100 px (was 120), the card's bottom padding 40 px (was 60), and four margins tightened by 2 to 4 px: about 70 px of air under the pill on a two-line synopsis, about 30 px on three. The layout rhythm e2e test now checks the three-line case.
2. Hero text starts at x 116 while rows start at x 80 (TV). Netflix aligns hero text with the row grid. **Left** (moving the text to 80 puts it 36 px inside the card's rounded edge).
3. Continue Watching leaves the right two thirds of the row empty with two items. Apple TV's Up Next does the same; acceptable.
4. Home rows: clean. The picked-up row (accent number, 16 px shift, hint) reads well.

## Second pass: the left items (2026-10-19)

**Search: which pattern.** Netflix's 2025 TV app puts Search in the top navigation and opens a search screen; Apple's tvOS search (WWDC20, session 10634) keeps the field and a one-line keyboard at the top with results right under it, widening to the full screen once focused; flystream (measured in `design/reference/flystream.md`) opens a centered command palette over a heavily blurred page. Marquee follows flystream's palette, as DECISION.md names it, with Apple's order of focus: the field first, Down to the scope pill, Down into the results. The navigation's Search opens it over the screen you are on (on TV, desktop and the phone's tab bar); Back, the scrim or a result closes it, and focus returns to Search on a remote. `/search` stays a deep link and shows the same panel as a page. The core's search view and rows are unchanged: rows keep their catalog heading, each result reads "Movie · 2019 · ★ 7.6". Genres are not shown: the core's search tiles carry none, and adding them is a core change.

**Episode stills.** No still, or one that fails to load, shows the series backdrop (else its poster) dimmed behind the episode number, as Apple TV and Netflix fill a missing still with show art.

**Home hero on the grid.** Netflix and Disney+ start the hero's words on the same line as the rows below. The card stays inset 44 px, as flystream's inset card; its words now start on the rows' grid line (the 80 px safe side), 36 px inside the card, clear of its 22 px corners. On a phone the grid line is the rows' 16 px gutter: the card's edges sit on it and the words keep 18 px inside the 26 px corners.

**Settings width.** One readable column (1400 canvas px, 876 CSS px on a 1440 desktop) centered under the navigation pill, as Netflix's and Apple's account pages, instead of a block at the left that left the right third empty. Pages opt in with `column`; Settings, Addons, Find addons, Debrid, Import, Home rows and the profile pages have it. Sync, pairing and Trakt (owned elsewhere) can add the same word.

**Small items.** A phone version row's quality starts on its size's line; Debrid's key page link sits centered on Save (a phone wraps it under Save); the selected edges and fills (season tabs, chips, Watched, pressed actions, the scope pill) come from the new `selected` tokens.

Each rule has an e2e assertion (`e2e/search.spec.ts`, `e2e/layout.spec.ts`) or a unit test (`tokens.test.ts`) and a mutation row in `bench/design/web-audit-mutations.jsonl`.

## Review round 1 (reviewer, 6/10), fixed

## Third pass: episode actions (2026-10-19)

**Before:** a focused episode row showed its two actions (Choose a version, Mark as watched) in a column beside the card, and the card narrowed to make room, so the list's right edge stepped in on every focus.

**Choice: the actions lay over the card's right end.** Two ways keep the width still: reserve an action column on every row, or show the actions inside the card. A reserved column takes about 240 px from every card all the time, to hold buttons only one row shows; Netflix's and Apple TV's episode rows never give up that width. So on TV and desktop the actions are a strip laid over the right end of the focused (or hovered) card, on strong glass, and the words beneath fade out instead of running under it. No card changes width or height, and nothing below moves. The phone keeps its round icon buttons beside the card, always shown.

**Focus order.** The strip sits inside the card's own box, where spatial search cannot see it, so the strip names its card (`data-fowner`): Right from the card enters the strip (Choose a version, then Down to Mark as watched), Left from either action returns to the card, and Back from an action returns to the card before it would leave the page. Up and Down from the card still move between episodes.

Checked by `TV: an episode's actions lay over its card` in `e2e/focus.spec.ts` (equal card widths with a row focused; Right, Down, Left, Right, Back) and four mutation rows.
