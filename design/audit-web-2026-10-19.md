# Web design audit, 2026-10-19

Every web screen captured headless on live data (the e2e core: Cinemeta, OpenSubtitles, Torrentio, the sample source) at TV 1920x1080, desktop 1440x900 and phone 390x844, then read against `design/DECISION.md` (Ember). Shots come from `apps/web/e2e/shots.spec.ts` with `SHOTS_DIR` and `SHOTS_PREFIX=audit-`; the after set is `apps/web/e2e/shots/audit-<screen>-<size>.jpg`. Sync and pairing were out of scope (under review).

Each screen lists its issues from the one that hurts the first impression most down, with what a top product (Netflix, Apple TV app, Disney+, flystream.net) does instead. Status: **fixed** (in this pass), **left** (ranked in the last section).

## Cross-screen

1. **Content slides under the floating navigation pill with nothing behind it** (Calendar opens this way; Home, Discover, Library, Settings once scrolled). Text sits on the same line as the pill and collides with it. Netflix and Disney+ put a scrim behind the top bar as soon as the page scrolls; flystream blurs. **Fixed**: a page-colored scrim fades in behind the pill once the page scrolls, and out at the top.
2. **Selected filter chips are white pills**, the look DECISION reserves for the one primary action. Discover shows three white pills at once (type, catalog, genre), Library two, Find addons two, Settings five. Apple TV and Netflix mark a selected filter with a lighter fill, never the primary button style. **Fixed**: a selected chip is a lighter fill with a bright edge and the check, the same as the season tabs.
3. **Focus can land under a fade or on the bottom edge on TV.** The episode list and the versions list fade their last 48 to 56 px, and a focused row there shows a cut ring; Settings opens with its focused row half below the screen. TV apps keep the focused item clear of the safe area. **Fixed**: focus scrolling honours a bottom scroll margin (the 60 px TV safe area, more above a fade).
4. **Raw colors outside the tokens**: the whole player (see Player), the browse title gradient end `#B9BDC6`. **Fixed** for both.
5. **Card edges are not one system**: link rows, addon cards and the facts table use the 1 px glass `edge`; the welcome doors and the versions pick card use a 2 px `line`, which reads as a different, flatter card. **Fixed**: one 1 px `edge` with the glass highlight.

## Home and Home rows

1. **The hero's eyebrow chip sits 17 px under the navigation pill on TV and desktop, and slides under it** when the synopsis runs to three lines (the hero stack is 482 px in a 600 px card whose top 100 px the pill covers). Netflix and flystream leave clear air between the top bar and the hero text. **Fixed**: the logo box is 100 px (was 120), the card's bottom padding 40 px (was 60), and four margins tightened by 2 to 4 px: about 70 px of air under the pill on a two-line synopsis, about 30 px on three. The layout rhythm e2e test now checks the three-line case.
2. Hero text starts at x 116 while rows start at x 80 (TV). Netflix aligns hero text with the row grid. **Left** (moving the text to 80 puts it 36 px inside the card's rounded edge).
3. Continue Watching leaves the right two thirds of the row empty with two items. Apple TV's Up Next does the same; acceptable.
4. Home rows: clean. The picked-up row (accent number, 16 px shift, hint) reads well.

## Discover, Library, Search (browse)

1. **Three white pills** (see cross-screen 2). **Fixed.**
2. **Chip rows that scroll sideways end in a hard cut mid-word** ("Horr" on desktop). Netflix and Apple TV fade the edge of a scroller so a cut item reads as more. **Fixed**: the last 96 px of a sideways chip row fade out (focus scrolling keeps the focused chip clear of it).
3. **The poster grid is start-aligned with a 160 to 220 px empty gutter on the right** (TV and desktop). Netflix's grids span the content width. **Fixed**: columns spread across the width.
4. Search is a full page with catalog rows, not the centered command palette DECISION describes. **Left** (a redesign, not a fix).
5. Posters without art show the name on a plain surface: good fallback, never a blank box.

## Title

1. **Phone: every episode carries two text pills ("Choose a version", "Mark as watched") under its card**, twenty pills on a ten-episode season. Netflix and Apple TV on phones put one small icon beside each episode. **Fixed**: on a phone the two actions are 44 px round icon buttons beside the card, with their words kept for screen readers; the still is 112 px wide (was 128) so titles keep their room.
2. **TV: a focused episode at the end of the list sits under the list's fade** (ring and bottom cut). **Fixed** (cross-screen 3).
3. **Phone: the "No playable version found" dot floats far left of its centered sentence.** **Fixed**: the wrapped reason reads as one left-aligned line with its dot.
4. Episodes without a still show a plain dark box with the number. Netflix shows the series art. **Left.**
5. The focused episode card narrows to make room for its two actions, so the right edge of the list steps in on focus. **Left** (deliberate pattern; a change touches the TV focus model).

## Versions

1. **The pick card's Play button is pinned to the card's foot**, 300 px below the release details on TV and desktop; the eye has to cross an empty panel to reach the primary action. Apple TV and Netflix put the action right under what it acts on. **Fixed**: the card hugs its content.
2. The pick card used a 2 px `line` edge (cross-screen 5). **Fixed.**
3. Phone: a row's size floats top right while its quality is centered. **Left** (minor).

## Player

1. **The player still runs the retired v1 palette**: cream text `#F6EFE6`, warm greys `#D9CEBF`, an amber focus ring `#FFB547`, an amber filled Skip on focus, warm brown panels `#1B1815` / `#2A251F`, a red `#FF7A6B` outside the tokens. It is the one screen that does not look like Marquee. DECISION: white text, the accent focus ring, round transport buttons, glass panels, and Skip as a white pill with an accent edge. **Fixed**: every color in `player.css` comes from the tokens; Skip is the white pill with an accent edge; panels, the up-next card and the toast are strong glass.
2. End card: the focused primary had the amber ring. **Fixed** with 1.

## Calendar

1. Content under the navigation pill when it opens on Today (cross-screen 1). **Fixed.**
2. **No skeleton while the schedule loads**: a spinner line and a blank page until the first day arrives. Netflix and Apple TV draw the shape of what is coming. **Fixed**: three skeleton days (date column and a still) while nothing has arrived.

## Settings, Addons, Find addons, Debrid, Import, Home rows

1. **Debrid shows three white primary pills** ("Sign in with a code" in every card). One primary per screen. **Fixed**: those are glass secondaries; the white pill stays for the step in progress (Save, Open).
2. **Addons on TV: "Paste a link" and "Find addons" touch** (no gap). **Fixed.**
3. Settings: the autofocused Addons row opened half below the screen on TV. **Fixed** (cross-screen 3).
4. Debrid: "Get a key at ..." sits 15 px above the Save button's center. **Left** (minor).
5. Import shows a live link code and QR. Fine for the product; the committed shots mask them (they are short-lived account link codes).
6. Settings pages use 1400 px wide lists on a 2300 px desktop canvas, leaving the right third empty. flystream's settings are as plain. **Left.**

## Onboarding (welcome)

1. **Step 2 has two white primaries**, Install and Continue. **Fixed**: Install is white only while the link field holds text; otherwise Continue is the one primary. The field and Install share a line, as on Addons (they were stacked with Find addons under them, three pills in a column).
2. **The step titles use -0.01em tracking**, every other display title -0.035em. **Fixed.**
3. The doors use a 2 px `line` edge (cross-screen 5). **Fixed.**

## Profiles

1. **Desktop: the picker sits in the top third**, not centered: it is centered in `100vh` (900 px) while the scaled desktop canvas is 1437 px tall. Netflix centers "Who's watching?". **Fixed**: centered in the canvas.
2. **TV: a focused profile shows two focus marks**, the accent outline around the whole tile and a white ring on the avatar. Poster tiles put one ring on the art. **Fixed**: the accent ring and halo sit on the avatar, the name turns accent, as on posters.
3. Manage and edit: clean.

## Left, ranked

1. Search as the centered command palette of DECISION (a redesign).
2. Episode stills: fall back to the series backdrop instead of a dark box.
3. Episode focus: keep the card width when its actions show (needs a TV focus model change).
4. Home hero text aligned to the row grid.
5. Settings family: use the desktop width (two columns, or a section list as flystream).
6. Versions phone row alignment; Debrid key-page link alignment.

The after runs used `MQ_E2E_PORT_OFFSET=100`, a new knob in `e2e/core.ts` (default ports unchanged), because another checkout's suite held the shared ports. The import screen's link code, QR and link are masked in the shots.
- `bunx tsc --noEmit` clean; `bun test src` 97 pass; e2e `flow`, `profiles`, `debrid`, `find-addons`, `perf` and `shots` (pairing excluded): 99 passed, no retries.
- Mutation check for the hero: with the old sizes the eyebrow overlaps the pill by 19.25 px on TV and 12.05 px on desktop under a three-line synopsis, and the layout rhythm test fails; with the fix it clears by about 33 px on TV.
- Calendar skeleton: seen live by holding the calendar's data back on the socket for 4 s (three skeleton days, then eight cards).
- Calendar's Today card shows its name card in the after shots: the live item for an episode airing today came with neither a still nor a poster (no image request failed), so that is the designed fallback, not a regression.
