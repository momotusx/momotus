# Design decision

## The v3 pick (2026-10-21)

The owner's words, translated: "I told you Apple TV: the colors and everything should be the same." So v3 is the direction closest to the Apple TV app, which both design critics named in every round: V2 Top Shelf (`design/exploration/v3/index.html`, five review rounds, 10 of 10 on TV and phone, `plans/reviews/design-v3-*`). Its colors follow Apple TV's own, as measured in `design/reference/apple-tv.md`: a black or near-black stage where the artwork is the only color, white text at Apple's opacity levels, 10 percent hairlines, a white focus (scale, lift, shadow, specular, never a colored ring), the white primary pill, and Apple's system blue only where Apple uses a key color (sheet primaries, sign-in, links). This replaces the blue-green accent switch of the exploration and the v2 accent #E5372A. If the owner wants another direction or a different key color, this block is where it changes.

Details decided with it, by the same rule (match Apple TV, except where the owner ruled a color out): switches use Apple's own green on iOS and Android (`#30D158` dark) as the iOS Settings switch does; destructive actions (Delete account, Remove) stay without red, as the owner ruled red out, and use the key blue in alerts with the destructive word itself; the TV form has no key color at all, as tvOS; provider sign-in buttons keep their provider's own colors (Facebook blue, Google's mark), since each provider draws its own button. The built exploration is `design/exploration/v3/v2.html`; the draft language and tokens are `design/exploration/v3/drafts/`.

## Owner direction, later on 2026-10-21

The owner's words, translated: study every page of Apple's design in detail, on the phone and on the web, and make our phone, web and desktop designs exactly that way; IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings sit side by side, nicely, by default, always; and take from Stremio's design too, which is not bad overall.

What this sets: phone layouts follow Apple's iPhone TV app (`design/reference/apple-tv.md`, iPhone sections); web and desktop layouts follow tv.apple.com as measured there (its navigation, hover and keyboard states, shelves and pages), not the TV canvas scaled; the TV form follows tvOS and is checked on Android TV; Stremio's good parts are studied in `design/reference/stremio.md` and borrowed where they fit this language; every title shows its IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings side by side wherever a rating appears (hero, title page, cards where ratings show), as Apple's TV app shows Rotten Tomatoes, by default and without any setting.

Addon text and emoji (2026-10-21): addons send their own text with emoji (a floppy disk before a size, flags before languages). The no-emoji rule covers everything Marquee shows, so the core strips emoji and pictographs from third-party text it passes through and keeps the words; flags become the language name the core already knows. The core's own captions stay as written. The 12 borrow items in `design/reference/stremio.md` are adopted where they fit the v3 language, as listed there.

## Owner direction for v3 (2026-10-21, before the pick)

The owner's words, translated: every page, Settings included, must look ultra sleek, Apple level, Apple style. Study Apple's own design in detail (the Apple TV app on tvOS and iPhone, tv.apple.com) and keep its appeal. Color: monochrome, with one accent between blue and green; no red and no orange. Do not mix colors. Run the design through review rounds until it is perfect.

What this sets for every v3 direction:
- Monochrome: black, greys and white carry the whole interface; the one accent is a blue-green (teal, mint or cyan family) used sparingly (focus, progress, the active marker, a selected state), never a large fill.
- No red or orange anywhere in the chrome. Delete and other destructive actions use the system's destructive style only where the platform requires one; the v2 accent #E5372A and the orange markers in the v3 exploration go.
- Apple's real layouts, type scale, materials and focus effects are the reference; the brand rules stay (own wordmark and icons, no Apple logos or artwork).

The pick between V1 to V5 is still the owner's; this block records the constraints all five must meet.

## v3 (current)

Status: v3, the owner's pick of 2026-10-21 (the two blocks above). The built frames are `design/exploration/v3/v2.html`; the rollout is `plans/design-v3-rollout.md`.

## The language

The Apple TV app's own look. A near-black stage where the artwork is the only color; white text at Apple's opacity levels; solid grey surfaces with 10 percent hairlines; fills instead of outlines; a white focus on TV that lifts the item and draws no ring; the white primary pill; Apple's system blue only where Apple uses a key color.

| Piece | Rule |
|---|---|
| Page | `bg` #1C1C1E (systemGray6). Dark only; `color.light` mirrors `color.dark` so the generators keep working. Full-bleed artwork fades into `bg` along Apple's layered ramps (a bottom ramp that always ends on `bg`, an 80 degree wash from the left, a light vignette) |
| Surfaces | Solid greys, no glass in the content: `surface` #2C2C2E (systemGray5) for cards, rows and groups, `raised` #3A3A3C (systemGray4) for the selected row and the keyboard keys. Glass only for controls that float over artwork or video: the 16 percent white fill of secondary buttons with a 20 px blur, on a ramp that keeps their white label at 4.5:1 over white art; the dots platter `rgba(0,0,0,.3)` with `saturate(1.8) blur(10px)`; the alert `rgba(30,30,32,.9)`; the player's audio and subtitle panel and the Up Next card over the picture, as Apple's player draws its menus |
| Lines | `line` and `edge` are a 10 percent white hairline (0.5 px on the phone, 1 px on TV). Every artwork carries a 25 percent white inner keyline (`lineStrong`) so dark art never melts into the page. Nothing else is outlined: no outlined cards, no edged buttons |
| Text | White by Apple's levels only: 92 percent (`text`, titles and body), 70 percent (`muted`, text on artwork and synopses), 64 percent (`dim`, secondary lines, captions, section headers), 40 percent (tertiary: chevrons, placeholders, the inactive dot). Pure white only on artwork (numerals, the active tab). No eyebrows over titles and no breadcrumbs: a pushed page has a back link (phone) or Back (TV) |
| Primary action | One per screen (Play, Resume, the first-run choice, Cancel in a destructive confirmation): a pill filled white 92 percent (`primary`) with a black 88 percent label (`onPrimary`), weight 600 |
| Secondary actions | Pills or circles filled white 16 percent with a pure white label (never the 92 percent `text` over art) and a 20 px backdrop blur; a value rides inside the pill at 70 percent ("Subtitles English") |
| Key color | Apple's system blue, phone and desktop only, never on TV. `key` #0A84FF for text and glyphs: links, the navigation bar's back and Edit, alert and confirmation buttons, "Not now", the list checkmark (4.66:1 on `bg`, 4.64:1 on the alert, 3.82:1 on `surface` as a glyph). `keyFill` #0071E3 with `onKey` white (4.70:1) for a sheet's primary button (Done on the six-digit sheet), as Apple's sign-in sheet draws it |
| Keyboard focus (desktop, phone, Mac) | The TV look below plus a solid 4 px `key` ring (`focus.ring`), 4.66:1 on `bg`, 3.82:1 on `surface`, 3.11:1 on `raised`; shown for keyboard focus only, never for a pointer or a touch |
| TV focus (Android TV and web TV) | White, never a color and never a ring: the item rises (scale 1.06 on cards, 1.03 on rows, 8 px up), its shadow grows to `0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`, and a specular sheen crosses its art (`linear-gradient(125deg, rgba(255,255,255,.26), .06 32%, 0 52%)`). Buttons and rows invert: white 92 percent with black 88 percent words and 64 percent secondary words. One focus per screen. A tile whose art is loading or missing lifts its placeholder the same way. tvOS keeps its v2 focus (the white ring and the scale) by the owner's decision of 2026-10-21; the TV form is set and checked on Android TV and web TV |
| Selected | A fill, not an edge. Segmented control: track `rgba(118,118,128,.24)`, the selected segment `control` #636366 with a white label. Tabs: the active one in a white 20 percent pill (TV) or white text and icon (phone tab bar, the rest 64 percent). The selected row in a list: `raised` |
| Progress | White on a dark track: the Continue Watching line (8 px TV, 4 px phone) on `rgba(0,0,0,.55)`, the player scrubber on white 20 percent. Never a hue |
| Switch | Apple's own, as iOS Settings draws it, on iOS and Android: 51x31, a 27 knob with a soft shadow; on is `switchOn` #30D158 (systemGreen, dark) with the white knob at the right (6.9:1 against `surface`), off is the 16 percent fill with the knob at the left. TV has no switch: a row shows On or Off as its value |
| Destructive | No red (the owner ruled it out). The destructive word carries the meaning: it is the plain secondary with its white word on every form (10.30:1), never the white primary and never `key` text on a fill (2.82:1); on TV, Cancel is the focused white primary. Web and Android confirm inline as today (press again, Keep or Delete); Apple uses its confirmation dialogs with no destructive role. Remove and Unlink are row values, never colored |
| Status and ratings | No status hues: `star`, `ok` and `bad` are white 92 percent and `warn` 70 percent, and every state reads from its words. A rating reads "IMDb 8.4" in text; Watched is a check glyph on a black 50 percent circle; a failed tile dims its art (`brightness(.4)`) and says so in the app's words |
| Type | Inter (OFL) on web and Android, tuned to SF's metrics; SF Pro (the system font) on Apple platforms, with SF's own tracking and Dynamic Type on iOS. Weights 700 display and headings, 600 labels, 400 body (500 for body on TV, as tvOS's Medium base), never below 400. Tracking on web and Android: -0.022em display, -0.019em headings, -0.011em labels. tvOS sizes on TV (76, 57, 48, 38, 31, 29, 25, 23; body 29, floor 23), iOS sizes on the phone (34, 28, 22, 20, 17, 15, 13, 12, 11) |
| Radii | Pills 999 for every button and tab; TV artwork, rows and the info card 20 (`l`), the TV info table and confirmation card 24; phone artwork and grouped lists 12 (`m`), web posters and cards 14 (`card`), the alert 14, a sheet's top corners 10 |
| Motion | 180 ms focus, 260 ms panels, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` on TV; the web stays opacity only, as Apple's site (artwork fades in, a press scales 0.1 s); reduced motion honoured |
| Brand | Own wordmark (Marquee, 700, no dot) and Lucide icons; no Apple logos, Apple artwork or SF Symbols |

Per form, by the owner's later direction (2026-10-21): the TV form follows the V2 frames; phone layouts follow Apple's iPhone TV app and web, desktop and the Mac follow tv.apple.com, both as measured in `design/reference/apple-tv.md` (navigation, hover and keyboard states, shelves, pages). The colors, type, focus and rules here hold on every form; the phone and desktop frames, and the numbers that differ there (the iPhone's #000 page and floating tab bar, the website's header and shelves), land before phase 1b of `plans/design-v3-rollout.md`.

## Patterns

- **Navigation.** TV and desktop: the wordmark at the left and a solid `surface` tab bar centered at the top (68 tall, its top at 46): Home, Discover, Library, Calendar, Search, Settings, each with its word (Search and Settings with icon and word); the active tab in a white 20 percent pill; the profile monogram, grey, at the bar's end. The bar shows where it shows today (tab roots and the Settings sub-pages). Phone: the iOS tab bar on `bg` with a 10 percent top hairline, five items split evenly (Home, Discover, Library, Search, Settings), icon over an 11 pt label, active white, the rest 64 percent. Calendar on the phone is its own section of Library: a row at the top of Library that pushes the calendar, with Library lit there, as Apple groups its library; a stored Calendar tab opens Library. Pushed pages show a blue back link at the top left (chevron and the parent's name), or a round glass back button over artwork.
- **Backdrops and scrims.** One piece per platform draws every full-bleed art: the hero ramps (bottom to `bg`, the 80 degree wash, the vignette), the phone shades, the legibility ramp under text on art, the TV player's bottom scrim, the phone player's top and bottom pair. Home, title, profiles, sign-in, the player and its end card all use it. Behind any row of secondaries the darkening is deep enough that a white label on the 16 percent fill keeps 4.5:1 over white art, measured at the label's top edge: about the page color at .72 behind the home and title button rows and black at .64 at the top of the player's pill row (the frame's own stops give 3.06:1 at the title's fourth button). A check computes it per position; the stops follow the check.
- **Home (the top shelf).** One full-bleed backdrop holds the first screen under the layered ramps. On its text column (left on TV and desktop, centered on the phone): the title logo, the genre line (600), two lines of synopsis at 70 percent, the meta line at 64 percent ("IMDb 8.4 · 2022– · 46 min"), the core's reason ("#1 in Popular Movies on Cinemeta") on Apple's explainability line (caption size, 600, 92 percent, 16 px above the buttons), then Play (white primary) and Details (secondary). Apple's capsule (a black 80 percent fill in a white gradient rim) is its "New" badge and waits for a source. Continue Watching rises into the ramp so the first row shows on the first screen.
- **Shelves and cards.** A shelf title in the `title3` role (38/700 TV, 20/600 phone), then a row with 40 px gaps on TV (10 on the phone). Continue Watching and movie rows are 16:9 backdrops (410x231 TV, 252x142 phone) with the title (600) and a 64 percent line under the art, never over it; series rows are 2:3 posters (217x326) on the HIG's 7-column grid. A title with no backdrop shows its poster, cropped to 16:9 from its upper part. Continue Watching carries the white progress line and the core's caption as is. Rows leave room for the lift and its shadow.
- **Title page.** A pushed page: the hero band (full-bleed backdrop, the logo on the text column, genres, synopsis, meta; the primary "Resume S6:E2" with "31 min left" inside it, then Trailer, In Library, Versions as labelled secondaries; the core's chosen-version line under the buttons at 64 percent). Below: Episodes with the season control (the segmented control on TV and desktop, the "Season 6" pull-down on the phone), the season's episode shelf of 16:9 lockups ("N. Title" and a 64 percent caption under the art), and All episodes at the shelf's end (the shelf's lockups play; a Calendar link, the one link that names a season, opens the title and then the Episodes page on that season, so Back returns to the title); then Cast as grey monograms (initials on `raised`, names under) and a `surface` info table holding only what the core gives (Genres, Runtime, Seasons, Episodes).
- **Episodes.** Its own page, opened by All episodes. TV and desktop: the logo and "Episodes" at the top, seasons as a list at the left (the selected one on `raised`), the season's episodes at the right: a 16:9 still with the progress line, the number and title (31/700), two lines of synopsis at 70 percent, "Watched · Feb 18, 2022" at 64 percent. The focused still grows toward its text, which never moves, and the focused row shows its own actions (choose a version, mark as watched) as secondaries under its text, as the focused addon does. Phone: a pushed page with the back link, "Episodes", the season pull-down and the rows, each with its actions as trailing round buttons.
- **Versions.** TV and desktop: the recommended version as a `surface` card at the left (kicker, headline "4K · HDR · Atmos", Size, Status, Via, the release name at 64 percent, the white Play); every other version as two-line `surface` rows by group at the right. The focused row lifts, turns white and spells out its release; the playing one carries a check (white on TV, `key` blue on the phone). Phone: an inset grouped list with uppercase section headers and the checkmark in `key`.
- **Player.** Paused: the picture stays whole under an eased bottom scrim; the title and "S6:E2 · Name" at the bottom left, the end time and quality at the bottom right; the round play button, elapsed and remaining time around the white scrubber; Audio, Subtitles, Versions, Speed as secondary pills with their values. The phone keeps the owner's rule: the scrubber at the safe foot and the options as one centered group under it, with Picture in Picture beside Close. The audio and subtitle panel and Up Next stay glass over the picture. The end card takes the backdrop piece (the title's art under the hero ramps), with Back as the white primary and Play again as a secondary, and no eyebrow.
- **Search.** The system keyboard on every TV (tvOS search, the Android TV keyboard, the TV browser's own); the field at the top left with recent searches under it, results as a 3 to 4 column grid of 16:9 cards with two-line titles, the count and source ("30 from Cinemeta") beside the heading. Phone: the iOS search field (the 24 percent grey fill, radius 10), then Movies and Series as two-column grids of 16:9 cards, each section marked with its addon (Cinemeta). Desktop: the palette on a solid `surface` panel.
- **Settings, Account, Addons, and every settings-like page.** TV: tvOS Settings, separate rounded `surface` rows (92 tall, 8 apart) at the right, a section header at 64 percent above each group, the page's title and the focused row's mark and line held large at the left; one focus target per row; a choice is a row with its value that opens a list, not a chip grid. Phone: iOS inset grouped lists on `surface`, 0.5 px hairlines inset past the icon tile, uppercase section headers and footers at 64 percent (Footnote 13), a chevron at 40 percent, the green switch, checkmarks in `key`, Edit as a blue link. Each addon is a two-line row (name and version, then what it provides) with its logo tile; on TV the focused addon shows its host and On, Up, Down, Remove.
- **Grids.** Discover, Library and other grids: a large title with no eyebrow, filters as the segmented control (two to four choices) or pull-downs (more), then 2:3 posters on the 7-column grid (TV) or two columns (phone), the rating as text under the art.
- **Flows.** Multi-step flows (pairing, import's code step, onboarding, recovery): one step per screen as the sign-in frame lays it out on TV (the line to follow at the left, the QR or the choice at the right) and as a page sheet on the phone (grabber, title, the step, its buttons pinned low).
- **Confirmation.** Destructive confirmations stay where they are today and take the destructive rule above: inline on web and Android; Apple's confirmation dialogs with `key` tint and Cancel; on TV, Cancel focused as the white primary.
- **Sign in.** TV first run: the line to follow at the left in Title 1 over a darkened backdrop, the pairing QR on white with its quiet zone at the right, a waiting line, the white primary to skip and a secondary for the recovery kit. Phone: the backdrop fading into `bg`, "Sign in" at 34/700, the three provider buttons as each provider draws them, a secondary for other ways, "Not now" as a blue link.
- **Six digits.** TV: the question in Title 2, six digits in cells filled white 10 percent, in two groups of three, Yes as the white primary, No as a secondary. Phone: a page sheet (grabber, radius 10 top corners, the page behind dimmed), the same cells on `surface`, Done in `keyFill` blue and "They differ" as a secondary, both pinned above the home indicator.
- **Profiles.** TV: the last watched title full bleed at 35 percent brightness, "Who's watching?" in Title 2, the profiles as large rounded grey tiles low and left (a lock glyph beside a protected name), Add profile as a `surface` tile, Manage profiles as a secondary. The focused tile rises and catches the sheen. Phone: the same tiles centered on `bg`. Each profile takes the next grey step on creation; there is no color choice. The editor is a settings-like page; PIN keys are `raised` keys and entered digits sit in the six-digit cells.
- **States.** Skeletons in the shapes they stand for (the full-bleed hero, 410x231 and 217x326 tiles); empty and failed states as text on `bg` (title at 92, message at 64, the action a secondary pill); toasts and banners on solid `raised` with a white glyph.

## Rules that stay (TV and accessibility)

- One token source: `design/tokens.json` (v3), generated to CSS variables (web), Compose and SwiftUI.
- TV canvas 1920x1080 points; body 29 pt, floor 23 pt; safe area 60 pt top and bottom, 80 pt sides.
- Focus: on Android TV and web TV the white lift (scale, rise, deeper shadow, sheen; buttons and rows invert to white), never a colored ring and never color alone; tvOS keeps its white ring and scale; desktop, phone and the Mac show the same look plus the 4 px `key` ring for keyboard focus only.
- The core's captions, labels and states are shown as is.
- Lucide icons with visible labels on TV; no emoji anywhere.
- Contrast floors: text 4.5:1, control edges, glyphs and the keyboard ring 3:1 (measured with the WCAG formula, a translucent color composited over what it sits on). The v3 pairs: text 92 on `bg` 14.51, 64 on `bg` 7.61, 64 on `surface` 6.65, 70 on `bg` 8.86, the primary label 14.47, white on the secondary fill 10.30, a focused row's value 6.33, `key` on `bg` 4.66, on `surface` 3.82, on `raised` 3.11, `key` on the alert 4.64, white on `keyFill` 4.70, `switchOn` on `surface` 6.89.
