Sign in to Marquee on that screen

This code signs a TV or computer in to your Marquee account. Only a phone with Marquee signed in can do that, by scanning it in the app.

  1. Get Marquee on this phone and sign in.
  2. Tap Sign in another device.
  3. Scan the code on the screen in front of you once more, and check that both show the same six digits.
