Momotus terms

These terms cover Momotus's sync service, the server your devices use to keep your library the same everywhere. Momotus works without it: an account is optional.

An early service

The sync service is new. It is offered as is, with no warranty of any kind: it may change, pause or stop, and it may lose data. Keep the recovery kit the app gives you, and do not rely on the service as your only copy of anything.

Your account

  • You sign in with Google, Facebook or your email address, or join from a device already in your account. Keep your devices and sign-ins safe: anyone holding them can use your account.
  • Use the service only for your own library. Do not try to break it, overload it or reach other people's data.
  • We may close an account that harms the service or other people.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. Without the app: the deletion page. What the service keeps, and for how long, is on the privacy page.
