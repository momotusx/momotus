Momotus privacy

This page covers Momotus's sync service, the server your devices use to keep your library the same everywhere. Momotus works without it: an account is optional.

What the sync service keeps

  • Your synced data: profiles, addons and their addresses, library, progress, watched marks and settings. Your devices encrypt it before sending it, and the service cannot read it. Only your devices hold the key.
  • For each device in your account: a public key, its encrypted name, and when it joined.
  • For each sign-in you link (Google, Facebook): a keyed hash of the account number that provider gives Momotus. Not your email, name, photo or any token from the provider.
  • For a sign-in with your email address: a keyed hash of the address, never the address itself. To send the code, your address and the code pass through our mail service, Amazon SES (Amazon Web Services, US East); we do not have it keep copies of the messages, and an address that bounces or complains may stay on its suppression list.
  • For abuse limits, a keyed hash of each address that asked for a code is kept for 24 hours.
  • To limit abuse: that keyed hash with the time of each Momotus account it created, kept 30 days.
  • For an account made without a sign-in: a keyed hash of the network it was made from (an IPv4 address, or the first 48 bits of an IPv6 one) with the time, kept 30 days, so one network can make only a few accounts a day.
  • While you sign in: a record of that sign-in, kept 10 minutes.

The service keeps no access log, shows no ads, has no analytics and sells nothing. It holds network addresses in memory only, to limit how often one address can call it and to tell whether a phone and a screen being signed in share a network, and writes none to disk: a sign-in in progress keeps a keyed hash of its address for 10 minutes. Cloudflare carries the traffic to it and sees addresses as part of that.

Outside the sync service: the streaming service logos in Discover load from TMDB's image servers (image.tmdb.org), which see your device's network address when they do.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. It is deleted from the sync service at once.

Without the app: sign in on the deletion page. That deletion runs after 7 days, so a stolen sign-in cannot delete your library at once.

Backups of the sync service keep an encrypted copy of a deleted account for up to 14 days more, then it is gone.
