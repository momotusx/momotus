Delete your Momotus account

Sign in with the account you use for Momotus. Deleting from this page takes 7 days, so a stolen sign-in cannot delete your library at once: until then, every device in your account shows the request in Settings, Account, with Cancel. To delete at once, use Settings, Account in the app. Backups keep an encrypted copy of a deleted account for up to 14 days more. Privacy

Continuing with Apple also turns off Sign in with Apple for Momotus at once, even if you stop before deleting.
